Handling Lista and Dictionaries in Robot Framework
--------------------------------------------------
Collections is Robot Framework's standard library that provides a set of keywords for handling Python lists and dictionaries.

Exercise 1
*** Variables ***
@{list1}    3       4       3

*** Test Cases ***
Example for Lis operators
        ${my_list}        Create List     1       2       3
        Log To Console      ${my_list}

        Append To List      ${my_list}      4
        Log To console      ${my_list}
